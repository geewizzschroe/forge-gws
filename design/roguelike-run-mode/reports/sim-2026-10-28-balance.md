# Balance pass, 2026-10-28

Single fights from 20 life with each starter deck: 20 per hero per encounter, 40 in all. The default AI plays both sides.
- **Precision:** at 40 fights a result is good to about ±15 points, so read close numbers as ties.
- **Encounter strength:** encounters fight as they are on floor 1. Elites, bosses and interlopers use their own life (25 or 30).
- **Starter decks:** a real run's deck is larger and stronger by the time it meets Elites and bosses. These numbers compare enemies with each other, not with a run.

## Out of line with their place

- **Every Elite is easier than most normal enemies** (58 to 80%). They're meant to be close to normal enemies or a little harder.
- **Gilgamesh** (58%) is far softer than the other bosses (2 to 20%).
- **Krenko** (2%) is far harder than the other bosses.
- **Goblin Raiders** (15%) plays like a boss at tier 2.
- **Summoner's Circle** (70%) plays like a tier 1 enemy at tier 2.
- **Malboro Marsh:** Lightning won 20 of 20 and Jill 6 of 20. The matchup matters more than the deck: Malboro's deathtouch is weak against first strike and lifelink.
- **The War Doctor:** Jill won 16 of 20 and Lightning 6 of 20.
