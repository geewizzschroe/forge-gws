## Summary

- **No run was won** (0 of 50, as before). The boss was reached **once** (batch A), against 6 times in the second report (1, 1 and 4).
- **Krenko's Warband was never beaten**: 0 of 14 fights (1 in a run, 13 single encounters), lasting 8 to 10 turns on average.
- **Goblin Raiders got harder for Jill**: 0% (A) and 11% (B) of fights in runs, against 33% and 27% before, and 0 of 8 single encounters. For Lightning it fell from 63% to 21%.
- **The Restless Dead**: Jill 67% of 3 (A) and 0% of 8 (B); Lightning 0% of 7 (was 10% of 20).
- **A Troop of Dwarves** is the most beatable: 33% (A), 47% (B), 50% (C).
- **No relics** were gained, and gold at the end averaged 1.5 to 24.5, so the shop's relics (60) and card removal (75) were seldom affordable.
