# Enemy roster, 2026-10-22

The enemies and bosses from `proposals/refinement-2026-10-22.md`, drafted as real content.
- Decks are 40 cards (60 for bosses), 2 to 4 copies of each card.
- Every card is in the card scope and none is quarantined.
- Every Base was checked in a real game (`EnemyBasesTest`).
- Random Encounter is shelved, so it isn't here.

## Things to watch

- **Krenko and Sephiroth** were almost never beaten by the AI hero (1 win in 32 fights). Whether that's right for a boss is your call. A person likely does better than the AI, especially with Jill's reactive cards.
- **Jumbo Cactuar:** one unblocked attack is 10,000 damage, which ends a run, since life carries over. Cactuar Desert is now tier 1, so it can be a first fight. Its Jumbo arrives around turn 7.
- **Card scope:** the boss decks reuse a few cards from the normal decks (Sephiroth and Vampire Court share some black cards; The Masamune is in both Sephiroth's and Gilgamesh's decks). There's no rule against it.

I stopped tuning there. Further passes would be chasing numbers that are inside the sample noise.
