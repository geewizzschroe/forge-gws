# Elites, 2026-10-23

Elites are enemies led by a Commander, with the deck and Base built around that Commander. In power they're meant to sit nearer normal enemies than bosses.

## The rules (your decisions, built)

- **Where they appear:** each fight above a floor's first row becomes an Elite at a 1 in 4 chance (`eliteChance`).
  - At most 3 a floor (`eliteMaxPerFloor`).
  - Each Elite at most once a floor.
  - Tiers don't apply to Elites.
  - They're marked "!" in orange on the map.
- **What they are:** 40-card decks with a Commander, 25 life, a 7-card hand.
- **What they give:** the normal gold, and a card reward of single cards (no buckets) in which every creature comes with an upgrade. Then a choice of 3 relics (`eliteRelicChoices`, PLACEHOLDER). Each relic slot there is rare only at the usual `rareRelicChance`.
- **Bosses give rare relics** instead of normal ones. A choice of 3 is drawn from the rare relics, and normal relics fill the choice only once the rare ones run out.

## Golbez (UB, built 2026-10-23, your design)

- **Commander:** Golbez, Crystal Collector (FIN, 1/4). Artifacts entering make him surveil. With 4 or more artifacts, each of his end steps returns a creature card from the graveyard to the hand.
- **Base, Tower of Zot:** "Crystal Hoard — Cycling abilities you activate cost {1} less. Whenever you cycle a card, create a Crystal token."
  - A Crystal is a colourless artifact token with "Sorcery spells you cast cost {1} less to cast." It's the mode's first custom token, in `tokens/`.
  - The Crystals are artifacts for Golbez.
  - The gag: his deck's only sorcery is Moment of Evil Triumph, so the Crystals exist to pay for it, and it shatters them.
- **Moment of Evil Triumph:** the mode's first custom card, a {8}{U}{B} sorcery: "Destroy all artifacts. Then return all creature cards from your graveyard to the battlefield."
  - It's a one-shot: the Crystals that paid for it are destroyed.
  - Artifact creatures it destroys (Gleaming Barrier, Magic Pot) come back with the rest.
  - The AI casts it once its graveyard holds 3 or more creatures (a new AI logic for the wipe, `BeforeReanimation`).
  - Custom cards live in `cards/` and are added to the card database when the content loads, the way Adventure adds its own.
  - It's listed as an exception to the card scope.
- **Deck:**
  - all five FIN landcyclers (now plain Cycling {2}): Ice Flan, Malboro, Hill Gigas, Balamb T-Rexaur, Cloudbound Moogle;
  - other cyclers: Scion of Darkness, Windcaller Aven, Choking Tethers;
  - artifacts: Gleaming Barrier, Magic Pot, Instant Ramen, and Bake into a Pie's Food;
  - no sorceries other than the Moment (2 copies);
  - graveyard fillers: Crow of Dark Tidings, Dreams of Laguna, Ultimecia;
  - Locke Cole and removal.
- **The challenge:** beat him before he reaches 10 mana, or hold up a counterspell.
- **AI:** Forge's AI already has a "Living End" plan (cycle everything, then reanimate). It now also switches on when the library or hand holds a card with the AI flag `AICyclingPlan`, which Moment of Evil Triumph carries.

**Calibration, before the Crystals** (single fights from 20 life, 12 per hero; the Base made Clues then): the hero won 23 of 24 games (Jill 92%, Lightning 100%).
- **Cycling:** the AI cycles a lot, about 5 to 22 Clues a game.
- **Golbez:** cast in only 12 of 24 games.
- **Moment of Evil Triumph:** cast in 1 of 24 games.
- **Game length:** games ended around the enemy's 6th to 9th turn, before it reached 10 mana. In practice the AI cycles away its board and loses before the plan comes together.

**Calibration with the Crystals:** same setup, 12 fights per hero.

| Version | Hero wins | Golbez cast | Moment cast (Golbez won) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crystals | 19 of 24 (79%) | 8 of 24 | 5 (5) |
| Crystals, and the AI holds its cycling until after its first main phase | 16 of 24 (67%) | 13 of 24 | 8 (8) |

- **The Moment decides the game:** when it resolves, Golbez has won every time so far. It came on his 6th to 8th turn.
- **Before the AI fix:** the AI spent all its mana cycling in its first main phase, and creatures, its commander included, wait for the second. Now it cycles only after its first main phase.

## Calibration

Single fights from 20 life, 12 per hero. The goal is "about normal-enemy hard": normal tier 2 and tier 3 enemies sit at roughly 30 to 45% hero wins.

**First pass:** the hero won very often against Halana (71%) and Tatyova (83%). The game logs showed the AI never cast either commander, for two reasons:
- **Late colours:** the land picker followed the 40-card library, so a commander's second colour came late. Halana's first Mountain was the 7th land.
- **Spell order:** when the commander was castable, the AI picked bigger creatures first.

**The fix:** two AI changes, which apply only in run mode.
- The land picker gets a commander's missing colour right after the first land, and weighs an uncast commander more.
- Casting the commander from the command zone gets top priority.

| Elite | Commander cast (of 24 games) | Jill | Lightning | Together |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ayli's Pilgrimage | 24 | 50% | 42% | 46% |
| Halana and Alena's Hunt | 21 (was 0 of 2 logged) | 83% | 83% | 83% |
| Tatyova's Tidepools | 22 (was 0 of 2 logged) | 75% | 58% | 67% |

- **Ayli** is in the normal-enemy range.
- **Tatyova** is easier than intended.
- **Halana** is still too easy even with her commander cast.
- **Halana and Tatyova changes:** both decks had one strengthening pass before the AI fix:
  - Halana: Brute Strength, Kindled Fury and Belligerent Guest out; Colossal Dreadmaw, Garruk's Packleader and Hill Gigas in.
  - Tatyova: Groundswell, Ixalli's Diviner, Aven Reedstalker and Capture Sphere out; Cackling Prowler, Bigfin Bouncer, Kapsho Kitefins and a third Elfsworn Giant in.
