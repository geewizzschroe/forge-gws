# New enemy concepts, 2026-10-23

Concepts only: no decks are built yet. The goal is to fill floor 2, which draws from tiers 2 and 3.

## The problem

Floor 2 draws only from tiers 2 and 3, which is 7 enemies (5 + 2), and each enemy can appear once per floor.
- On 200 generated floor 2 maps, every one of the 7 was used on nearly every map (7.0 fights per map).
- About 3.5 fight nodes per map became Events instead. On floor 1 it's about 0.4.

These five concepts would add 2 to tier 2 and 3 to tier 3, making 12 enemies for floor 2. That should cover a floor 2 map, but I'd measure it once they're built.

They also fill colour gaps. No normal enemy is blue now (only the Kefka boss), and none is BR, UR, UB, WU or RG.

## At a glance

| Tier | Encounter | Colours | Identity | Base |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Sky Pirates | UR | Raid and Treasure | The Strahl: their combat damage to you makes a Treasure |
| 2 | Shinobi of Wutai | UB | Ninjutsu tempo | Smoke Bomb: one of their creatures can't be blocked each combat |
| 3 | Magitek Research Facility | WU | Artifacts and Vehicles | Assembly Line: a Thopter each end step with 3+ artifacts |
| 3 | The Battle Square | RG | Huge beasts | Main Event: their 5-power creatures have trample |
| 3 | Black Mage Village | BR | Noncreature spells ping you | Black Mage Choir: keeps one pinging Wizard token around |

Every card named below is in the card scope and not quarantined (checked with the scope tools). The AI already handles ninjutsu and crewing Vehicles.

## Tier 2

### Sky Pirates (UR)

FFXII's sky pirates, with Foundations' Pirates as the crew.
- **Identity:** cheap evasive attackers, Raid payoffs and Treasure, so their mana gets ahead of yours if you let them connect.
- **Base, "The Strahl":** "Plunder: whenever one or more creatures they control deal combat damage to you, they create a Treasure token."
  - Scripted as a combat damage trigger, once per combat.
- **Signature cards:**
  - Vaan, Street Thief (R, rare)
  - Captain Lannery Storm
  - Corsair Captain
  - Kitesail Corsair
  - Storm Fleet Spy
  - Fathom Fleet Firebrand
  - Goblin Boarders
  - Adventurer's Airship
- **Left out for colour:** Balthier and Fran (RG).
- **To watch:** Skyship Buccaneer would fit, but it's in Jill's starter. Sharing cards isn't against any rule; I left it out so the fight doesn't mirror her deck.

### Shinobi of Wutai (UB)

Ninjas, after Yuffie's home in FFVII.
- **Identity:** small evasive creatures swapped for better ones mid-combat (ninjutsu).
- **Base, "Smoke Bomb":** "At the beginning of combat on their turn, target creature they control can't be blocked this turn."
  - This guarantees a ninjutsu window each turn without the deck needing many evasive creatures.
- **Signature cards:**
  - Ninja of the Deep Hours
  - Prosperous Thief
  - Moonsnare Specialist
  - Phantom Ninja
  - Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow (rare)
  - Ninja's Blades
  - Reno and Rude (FFVII's Turks; their combat damage trigger fits)
- **Left out for colour:** Yuffie, Materia Hunter (R).
- **To watch:**
  - Moon-Circuit Hacker is in Jill's core.
  - The Base gives one unblockable attacker every turn, which may be a lot at tier 2. If it is, the fallback is "power 2 or less".

## Tier 3

### Magitek Research Facility (WU)

FFVI's Empire.
- **Identity:** artifact creatures and Vehicles that grow out of control.
- **Base, "Assembly Line":** "At the beginning of their end step, if they control three or more artifacts, they create a 1/1 colourless Thopter artifact creature token with flying."
  - Same pattern as Ancient Grove's five-Elf check.
- **Signature cards:**
  - Magitek Infantry
  - Magitek Armor
  - Cid, Timeless Artificer
  - Edgar, King of Figaro (rare)
  - Cargo Ship
  - Adventurer's Airship
  - Thopter Fabricator (rare)
  - Skysovereign, Consul Flagship (mythic; at most 2 copies, maybe 0)
- **Left out for colour:** Celes, Rune Knight (RWB) and Edgar, Master Machinist (RW).
- **To watch:** Skysovereign deals 3 damage to a creature on entering and on each attack, so it's a contender for "too strong at tier 3".

### The Battle Square (RG)

The Gold Saucer's arena in FFVII.
- **Identity:** few but huge creatures. You have to answer each one or chump it.
- **Base, "Main Event":** "Creatures they control with power 5 or greater have trample."
- **Signature cards:**
  - Red XIII, Proud Warrior (rare)
  - Coliseum Behemoth
  - Hill Gigas
  - Giant Cindermaw
  - Ravenous Giant
  - Garruk's Packleader
  - Ripjaw Raptor (rare)
  - Thrashing Brontodon
  - Elder Gargaroth (mythic; maybe 0 or 2)
- **To watch:**
  - The curve has to work under the landless rules. A deck of 5- and 6-drops may stall early.
  - Several Foundations beasts use landfall (Baloth Woodcrasher, Rampaging Baloths). I left them out because I don't know how landfall behaves with the land picker.

### Black Mage Village (BR)

FFIX's black mages.
- **Identity:** noncreature spells, each one pinging you.
- **Base, "Black Mage Choir":** "At the beginning of their first main phase, if they control no Wizard, they create a 0/1 black Wizard creature token with 'Whenever you cast a noncreature spell, this creature deals 1 damage to each opponent.'"
  - Same pattern as Chocobo Ranch's "one Bird".
  - It's the token Kuja and Vivi's Persistence make.
- **Signature cards:**
  - Kuja, Genome Sorcerer (rare)
  - Black Waltz No. 3
  - Vivi's Persistence (rare)
  - Cornered by Black Mages
  - Black Mage's Rod
  - Blazing Bomb
  - Crackling Cyclops
  - burn and removal from Foundations
- **Left out for colour:** Vivi Ornitier (UR, mythic). He's the most famous black mage, so you may want the village to be UBR instead, as Kefka is three colours.
- **To watch:** spell-heavy decks are where the AI is weakest, so this one may play below its tier.
